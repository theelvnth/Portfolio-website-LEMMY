The Philosophy

Each portrait exists in two states: the unmasked — the composed exterior we present to the world — and the masked — where abstract neoexpressionism reveals the hidden emotions beneath the surface.

The skull, ever-present in my work, serves not as a symbol of death, but as the universal foundation beneath every face — the truth we all share.
